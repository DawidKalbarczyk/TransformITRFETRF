How to use this tool?

Text Area:

Instructions:

1. Enter coordinates as follows: "Point_ID;X;Y;Z".

Example: WAT1;3655222.526;1396074.975;5020262.786;

2. Each point must be on a new line.

3. Spaces, tabs and commas are acceptable.

4. Do not provide any excess information
